About Kael Beauty Centre

Welcome to Kael Beauty Centre

Located on Earls Court Road in London, Kael Beauty Centre is a bright and spacious unisex salon offering a wide range of hair and beauty services. From massage and waxing to facials, nails, brows, lashes and laser hair removal, every treatment is delivered with care and attention to detail.

Whether you are looking to relax with a massage, refresh your nails, shape your brows or try a new beauty treatment, our friendly team is here to help you leave feeling confident, refreshed and beautifully looked after.
